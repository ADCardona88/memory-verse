# Review step

Act as a strict reviewer of my latest change for step N of our plan. Don't edit anything.

Tell me in plain English: does it do what the step says, does it break any of our project rules, does it include anything we didn't need, and will it be easy to use on an iPad?

Take **N** from the arguments after `/review-step` (e.g. `/review-step 1`). If missing, ask.
